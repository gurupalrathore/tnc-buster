SCSE4041 Mini Project · Plain-Language Legal Literacy

Understand what you agree to, before you click “Accept”.

Ordinary users rarely have time to decode dense legal agreements. T&C Buster breaks down Terms of Service and Privacy Policies into plain-English summaries, evidence-backed risk flags, and a human-reading checklist so every AI claim can be verified against exact text.

Try Interactive Preview View Full-Release Roadmap

Built Around Three Techniques

  • 01
    Prompt Chaining

    Separates clause extraction, plain-language risk analysis, and quote verification into 3 focused LLM steps.

  • 02
    Structured Output

    Produces predictable JSON covering Summary, Obligations, Risk Flags with severity, and Questions.

  • 03
    Human Accuracy Check

    Matches each risk flag to its exact source quote so humans can verify accuracy in seconds.
